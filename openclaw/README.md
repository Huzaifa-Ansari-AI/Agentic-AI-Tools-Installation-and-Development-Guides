# OpenClaw

## What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is a self-hosted AI gateway that connects your favorite messaging apps—Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage, Slack, and 20+ others—to an always-available AI assistant. You run a single Gateway process on your own machine (or server), and it becomes the bridge between your chats and a coding-focused AI agent.

Think of it as your personal AI operations center that lives on your computer and responds to messages from anywhere.

## Why Use OpenClaw?

- **Self-hosted**: Your data and conversations stay on your machine. No cloud vendor lock-in.
- **Multi-channel**: One Gateway serves all your messaging apps at once. Chat from WhatsApp, Discord, or Telegram—same agent.
- **Always available**: The Gateway runs 24/7 and remembers conversations, preferences, and workflows across sessions.
- **Built for coding**: Designed for developers who need an AI that can write code, run commands, and automate tasks.
- **Hackable**: Extend with community skills or write your own. OpenClaw can even create new capabilities on its own.
